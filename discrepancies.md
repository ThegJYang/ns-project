# Discrepancies found in audited papers

## D1

**Paper:** Liu, Xin, Ye, Yin, *A Note on Generalized Repunit Numerical Semigroups*, [arXiv:2306.10738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10738)

**Section/lemma in paper with error:** Corollary 4.2, Section 4 (genus formula only — the Frobenius formula in the same corollary is fine)

**Lemma statement:** For the repunit numerical semigroup $S(b,n)$,
$$g(S(b,n)) = \frac{b^n}{2}\left(\frac{b^n-1}{b-1} + n - 1\right)$$

**Correction:** The last term should be $n-2$:
$$g(S(b,n)) = \frac{b^n}{2}\left(\frac{b^n-1}{b-1} + n - 2\right)$$

**Short explanation:** Corollary 4.2 is supposed to be the $d=1$ special case of the paper's own Theorem 4.1, but specializing Theorem 4.1 correctly gives $n-2$, not $n-1$. The printed version is too big by exactly $b^n/2$ — for $b=2, n=3$ (generators $\langle 7,15,31\rangle$) it says 36 when the true genus is 32. It also gives non-integer answers when $b^n$ is odd (e.g. 22.5 for $b=3, n=2$), which is impossible for a count of gaps.

## D2

**Paper:** Arias, Borja, Rhenals, *The Frobenius problem for numerical semigroups generated by tails of sequences of the form $ca^n - d$*, [arXiv:2111.04899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04899)

**Section/lemma in paper with error:** Example 5.2(2), Section 5 — the family $x_t = 5\cdot 3^t - 1$

**Lemma statement:**
$$F\!\left(\tfrac12 S_n\right) = 5\cdot 3^n R_n + 1 \qquad \text{where } R_t = \tfrac{3^t-1}{2}$$

**Correction:**
$$F\!\left(\tfrac12 S_n\right) = \frac{25\cdot 3^{2n} - 5\cdot 3^n - 2}{2}$$
giving 104 at $n=1$ (generators $\langle 7,22,67,202\rangle$) and 989 at $n=2$ (generators $\langle 22,67,202,607,1822\rangle$), versus the printed 16 and 181.

**Short explanation:** Only the final simplification is wrong — everything before it is correct. The paper's own expression $\tfrac12(2s_n + s_{n+1} - s_0)$ gives the right answer one line earlier, so the paper contradicts itself. The likely cause: the printed formula puts the plain repunit $R_n$ where $s_0/\lambda$ belongs. Those two are equal in the previous example (5.2(1), where $c=1$), but not here, where $c=5$.

## D3

**Paper:** Arias, Borja, Rhenals, *The Frobenius problem for numerical semigroups generated by tails of sequences of the form $ca^n - d$*, [arXiv:2111.04899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04899)

**Section/lemma in paper with error:** Section 5, family $x_t = (a^k-1)a^t - 1$ with $a \ge 3$, sub-case $n > 1$, $k \ge 2$, $k-2 \ge (a-1)R_n$

**Lemma statement:**
$$F(S_n) = a^{2n+2k+1} - a^{2n+2k} - a^{2n+k+1} - a^{2n+k} - a^{n+k} - a^{n+1} + 2a^n - 2a + 3$$

**Correction:** Add the missing term $+\,2a^{n+k+2}$:
$$F(S_n) = a^{2n+2k+1} - a^{2n+2k} - a^{2n+k+1} - a^{2n+k} - a^{n+k} - a^{n+1} + 2a^{n+k+2} + 2a^n - 2a + 3$$

**Short explanation:** The printed formula is always too small by exactly $2a^{n+k+2}$. Checked at $a=3, n=2$ for three values of $k$: at $k=10$ it gives 564,839,409,633 against a true 564,848,975,571 (short by $2\cdot 3^{14}$), and at $k=11$ and $k=12$ it is short by $2\cdot 3^{15}$ and $2\cdot 3^{16}$. A clean pattern like that points to a term dropped during the derivation rather than an arithmetic typo. Unlike D2 there is no correct intermediate expression elsewhere in the paper, so this printed formula is the only statement available for this sub-case.
